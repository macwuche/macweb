Modular Homes

Tesla's tiny house is a modular, sustainable, and foldable home that's designed to be lightweight, portable, and environmentally conscious:

Materials

The house is made from sustainable, locally-sourced, chemical-free timber, concrete steel, and EPS foam. The hardwood exterior is laminated to make the house more structurally sound and insulated.

Size

The house is 20 ft x 7 ft x 13 ft and weighs 1,800–2,200 kg. It's designed to be portable and can be transported by truck or trailer.

Energy

The house is powered by a 2 kW solar system and Powerwall, which can store energy to use at night. The house can also be monitored and controlled with a Tesla app.

Features

The house includes a queen-sized bed, washroom, kitchen appliances, and a studio. It can withstand extreme conditions like category 5 hurricanes and earthquakes measuring 8–9 on the Richter scale.

Customization

The house can be customized in 15 minutes when ordered. The app allows users to control the house, see what's going on when they're not home, and get notifications.

Solar Roof/Panel

Install Solar Roof and power your home with a fully integrated solar and energy storage system. The glass solar tiles and steel roofing tiles look great up close and from the street, complementing your home’s natural styling.

Energy Storage

Powerwall is a compact home battery that is bundled with Solar Roof, providing you with 24/7 energy security. It stores the energy you produce with Solar Roof so you can power your home anytime—at night or during an outage.

Durability

Solar Roof is comprised of both glass solar tiles and steel roofing tiles. Glass solar tiles produce energy, while architectural-grade steel tiles add longevity and corrosion resistance to your roof. Both are durable, strong and engineered for all-weather protection. With a 25-year warranty, Solar Roof will continue to produce clean energy and protect your home for decades to come.

Installation

Our in-house team of energy professionals has installed nearly 4.0 GW of solar across approximately 480,000 roofs—cumulatively generating over 25.0 TWhs of clean energy. From design to power on, we take care of everything. Chat with a Tesla Advisor to ask any questions about Solar Roof.

Efficiency

Generate the most energy possible, even on roofs with complicated angles and intermittent sunlight. Glass solar tiles and architectural-grade steel tiles, vent covers and ridge caps come together to form a roof that is both durable and powerful.
